Food to gather around

Hope is built around fire, spice and good company. The menu takes flavours many of us grew up with and gives them a modern turn: coconut and tamarind, charcoal and citrus, greens and grains.

Come for a long lunch, a quick coffee or a table full of friends. There is something on the menu for everyone, including vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free guests.
